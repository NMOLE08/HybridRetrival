# Phase 2 provisional dataset card

This is an **unreviewed draft**, not a frozen benchmark or a basis for an effectiveness claim. Stage 14 remains provisional and its gate is not met.

## Contents and construction

The draft has 150 source-backed passages: 90 published CVE CNA descriptions and 60 contiguous source-code chunks from 10 Espressif `soc_caps.h` files. Each of the 10 files is split into six sequential chunks; a chunk can omit surrounding preprocessor context, so consult the full retained source when interpreting it. The hardware sample reuses the 10 Phase 1 source files; the 90 CVE records exclude all Phase 1 corpus CVE IDs. Documents are shared across query splits by retrieval design. Raw source files, exact pinned revision, SHA-256, source URL, extraction and rights are in `data/phase2/sources/`, `provenance.csv`, `NOTICE.md` and `outputs/phase2/manifests/source_collection.json`.

Thirty-six CVE records were targeted by prewritten draft questions. The 54 additional distractors were selected from seeded filenames in the 2022–2024 `0xxx` folders, 18 per year; this convenience sample is not representative of CVEs. The first attempt inadvertently selected all 54 from 2022; its manifest, corpus, provenance and review output are retained under `outputs/phase2/drafts/`, and the source ordering was corrected before retrieval or judgment. Espressif chunks are code rather than independent prose sources. The resulting 90/60 and 150 total are target counts, not claims of sample diversity or statistical power. Retained source notices and exact file SPDX headers are documented in `data/phase2/NOTICE.md`; do not infer license terms for other Espressif documentation from source-code terms.

There are 70 agent-authored question-first drafts: 24 development (18 identifier, 6 natural), 46 proposed new test (34 identifier, 12 natural). They were checkpointed before the new CVE record contents were downloaded or any Phase 2 ranking was viewed. Some source files were already available from Phase 1; queries are not independently human-authored and may reflect prior general knowledge or bias. The draft test is not yet locked and no Phase 2 retrieval, parameter selection, or evaluation has been run. CVE entities and ESP32 variants are assigned to one query split, with docs shared. The Apache CVE-2021-41773 / CVE-2021-42013 pair is in the same split. Exact topic IDs and query strings have no cross-split overlap, but this does not prove semantic separation.

## Review and known shortcomings

The source-evidence package contains 241 candidate pairs across 70 questions. The proposed and human relevance columns are unjudged or blank; there are **zero human-reviewed pairs**. Candidate selection used direct CVE references, numeric neighbors and hardware macro-name lookup, not model rankings. Candidates are not known positives or negatives. Three identifier questions (`esp-08`, `esp-11`, `esp-13`) do not have the expected macro in their target source and require additional evidence or revision; absence is not a negative answer. Two Phase 2 **development** questions (`esp-01`, `esp-02`) resemble inspected Phase 1 test questions (token Jaccard 0.6364); do not use them for tuning without independent revision and a new overlap audit. There are only two query-level explicit cross-reference candidates, below the five-case target. The initial code-word near-miss candidates have not been judged or verified as genuinely confusable. Forty or more independent test-query topics, human-labeled coverage, and answerable known positives have not been established.

`outputs/phase2/manifests/dataset_validation.json` records source hash checks and automated overlap flags. Its successful structural checks do not establish semantic validity, comprehensive labels, query independence or a passed stage gate. One console inspection of CVE descriptions failed on a Windows console encoding error; the raw source JSON was retained and is not corrupted. No final qrels or reviewed labels exist.
