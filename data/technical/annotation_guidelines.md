# Pilot annotation protocol

This benchmark is a **single-agent curated pilot**, not independent human-reviewed ground truth. Queries were authored against source passages and judged before retrieval comparisons; this construction can favor the originating source. Do not describe their origin as human-authored or human-reviewed. The `agent_authored` origin is an explicit honest extension of the plan's origin vocabulary.

A relevant passage directly addresses the entity and requested attribute or mechanism. A passage about a different supported variant is not relevant merely because its terminology is similar. A source mentioning multiple identifiers may be relevant if it substantively addresses the requested one; test01 has two positives after this cross-reference was reviewed. For natural queries, an entity need not be named in the query but must directly address the information requested.

Source passages and query text were reviewed against the 31-passage pilot during construction. Exact CVE mentions were scanned across all titles and passage texts, and the multi-ID Log4j record was specifically adjudicated. The ten chip passages are literal per-chip capability lines with their chip names supplied as titles. Selected hard negatives are recorded explicitly as 0 with audit notes; all other unlisted query-document pairs are unjudged. Positive judgments and selected negatives are in the qrels and evidence CSVs; unjudged does not mean irrelevant. One-positive queries are not proof that other relevant passages do not exist in a larger corpus.

Dev and test do not share query IDs or exact text; requests target different entities or information needs. Cross-split topics and identifier families necessarily overlap in this small collection, and token overlap is only a crude leakage screen. Independent adjudication and larger, topic-separated datasets are needed for credible generalization. Freeze the test queries and qrels before tuning; do not change them in response to test results.
